HYBRID COMBAT (Elden Ring weight + Wukong fluidity + WoW depth):

Every move has a state diagram, event timeline, cost, recovery, hitbox
window, cue, input buffering, server-authority mapping, and tests.

COMBAT FEEL (Game Feel):
- Hit-stop: 0.05-0.15s freeze on hit contact (scales by weapon weight)
- Camera shake: scales by hit weight (small for light, heavy for boss)
- Input buffering: 0.15s window (inputs during recovery queue)
- Combo chaining: 0.20s window, max 5 hits, chains scale damage
- Dodge i-frames: 8-13 frames invulnerable during dodge
- Parry window: 3 frames for perfect parry, 6 for partial
- Slow-motion: 0.3x on perfect dodge, 0.4x on critical hit
- Stamina management: costs on dodge, sprint, abilities, attacks
- Poise/Stagger: poise stat, break = stagger = open for crit
- Animation commitment: heavy attacks lock longer than light
- Footstep audio synced to animation
- Weapon trail VFX on swing

MOVEMENT:
- Camera-relative locomotion (WASD + mouse)
- Sprint (hold shift)
- Jump (spacebar)
- Dodge: 8-direction grounded, 100-160ms input buffer,
  2.5-4m travel, 24-34 frames total, 8-13 i-frames at 60fps.
  Stamina cost on activation (once). Fails safely if no stamina.
  Dodge only from idle/recovery/approved cancel windows.
  NO universal cancel — cannot dodge during committed attacks or CC.
- Perfect Dodge: Triggers when a threatening attack passes through
  a narrow timing window. Plays temporal afterimage VFX.
  Modest reward (small damage boost). Cooldown + anti-spam.
  Visual afterimage ≠ true invulnerability.

COMBAT:
- Light attack (left click) — chains into combos (3-5 hits)
- Heavy attack (right click) — longer commitment, higher damage
- Charge attack (hold) — wind up for bonus damage
- Block (right-click hold with shield) — reduces damage
- Perfect Parry (block within 3-frame window) — deflects, opens guard counter
- Guard Counter — heavy attack after successful block/parry
- Lock-on (middle mouse) — target lock for melee
- Stagger/Poise — poise stat determines if hit interrupts.
  Poise breaks → enemy staggers → open for crit
- Weapon Arts (class-specific signature move)

NO AUTO-ATTACK. Every action is manual input. High skill ceiling.

1. OFFLINE FIRST — Complete game playable solo. MMO is additive.
2. ATMOSPHERIC PRESENCE (SIGNATURE ECLIPSE FEATURE) -
   Powerful characters have visible auras and environmental
   effects that other players can SEE and FEEL. This is unique
   to Eclipse - no other MMO has this. When a high-level player
   enters an area, other players feel the pressure through:
   - Visible aura shimmering with elemental energy around the player
   - Ground cracks with fracture lines beneath their feet
   - Ambient particles (embers, frost, shadow wisps) emanating from them
   - Local lighting shifts (warmer for Fire, colder for Ice, etc.)

   - Weapon leaves a faint trail of light when drawn

   NO screen desaturation. NO forced trembling/kneeling.
   NO hesitation delay or input lag.
   The pressure is FELT through atmosphere, not mechanics.
   Other games dont have this. Eclipse does.

4. HIGH SKILL CEILING — Perfect dodge, perfect parry, combo chains,
   timing windows reward skilled play. But not so hard that casual
   players can't enjoy the story.
5. FAIR PvP — Gear matters but skill matters more. A skilled Epic-
   geared player CAN beat a Legendary-geared player. No mechanic
   makes this impossible.
6. ORIGINAL IP — No copying Elden Ring, Wukong, WoW assets, code,
   animations, audio, or UI. Study their principles, build our own.
7. BLACK REPRESENTATION — Full melaninated skin as default across
   all ancestries. Authentic textured hair (locs, braids, twists).
   All skin tones available in character creation.
   AETHERBORN SPECIFIC: White hair, deep melanin skin,
   luminous fracture markings, crystalline energy wings.
8. HONEST STATUS — Never claim a system is "done" because source
   files exist. Done = compiles + runs in PIE + tested + evidence.
9. ONE CANON — This document. If a prior PDF contradicts this,
   the PDF is wrong. If the C++ code contradicts this, fix the code.
10. NO RETCONS — Lore is locked. What happened happened. New
    content expands the world; it doesn't rewrite what's established.
