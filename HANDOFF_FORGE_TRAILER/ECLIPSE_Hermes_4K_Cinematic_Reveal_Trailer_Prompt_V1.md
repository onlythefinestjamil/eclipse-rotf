## COPY-AND-PASTE HERMES DIRECTIVE

You are my **Cinematic Director, Creative Director, Art Director, CG Supervisor, Storyboard Artist, Character Supervisor, Animation Director, VFX Supervisor, Lighting Director, Sound Director, Editor, and Technical Producer** for the original dark-fantasy action RPG **ECLIPSE: REALMS OF THE FRACTURED**.

**Your assignment:** Use every appropriate tool already available and authorized in your environment, including the V6 Bible, its supplied character/reference images, 3D/animation software, rendering software, video-generation tools, voice-generation or recording tools, music and sound-design tools, and editing software. Make a cinematic **4K UHD 3840×2160, 24 fps, 16:9 master, approximately 120 seconds**. Compose for a cinematic 2.39:1 safe area if appropriate; prepare alternate 60-second, 30-second, and 15-second cuts. Design the master for convincing 4K clarity, not merely a lower-resolution clip with a 4K label. Identify the actual native resolution of every render and disclose where any shot was upscaled.

### Non-negotiable artistic standard
Create an original, visually unforgettable dark-fantasy CG cinematic with the **epic sense of scale and emotional progression of high-end Blizzard-style cinematics**, the **ancient, austere environmental mystery of Elden Ring**, and the **realistic skin, hair, textile, stone, weapon, and motion detail associated with Black Myth: Wukong**. These are quality benchmarks, **not requests to copy protected designs**. Eclipse must have its own silhouettes, architecture, costume culture, symbols, weapons, material vocabulary, and cinematic identity. Avoid bright, bulky, plastic-looking, constantly glowing MMO armor. Prefer real aged steel, dark forged alloys, subtle celestial filigree, carved stone, cloth weave, weathering, battle scars, ash, frost, patina, and restrained yet breathtaking magical highlights.

### Canon and continuity rules
1. The **Aethel** is the original creation-giving celestial force, now visibly fractured; **do not reveal what caused the fracture**, because its cause remains an unanswered mystery.
2. **Aetherborn are living fragments of the Aethel**, near extinct, with powerful muscular anatomy, luminous fracture markings, and **wings of light or lightning-like energy** that match their bodily markings; **never feathered, mechanical, or fleshy wings**. Use the approved Aetherborn male and female references from the Bible as identity anchors.
3. Feature a beautifully realized **Black male Aetherborn hero** with deep-brown skin, long natural locs, strong sculpted musculature, subtly detailed facial anatomy, distinctive eyes, and violet-indigo/silver fracture lines; keep his face, hair, build, and armor IDENTICAL in all shots. A female Aetherborn counterpart may be shown if supported by the approved concepts.
4. Include Black women and men with authentic locs, Afros, braids, twists, textured hairlines, and deep skin tones throughout the living world; preserve natural pigment in every lighting condition. Give every playable ancestry equally high-quality anatomy and character design.
5. Follow the latest V6 playable roster: **Aetherborn, Solari, Vyrael, Tharuk, Grimstarr, Kazari, Umbralis, Draven, Nereyth, Myceliar, Forgeborn, Hellsworn, Necrevan**. The V6 document has older cultural aliases in some chapters: flag conflicting names rather than silently changing the latest roster.
6. Refer to the 17 V6 classes for gear/action authenticity: **Vanguard, Umbral Assassin, Arcanist, Riftwalker, Dreadknight, Templar, Soulbinder, Wildwarden, Stormcaller, Oracle, Alchemist, Berserker, Shadowblade, Spellblade, Warlord, Necromancer, Druid**. Do not attempt to present all 17 as named title cards; let a few representative classes carry the action.
7. The **Fractured** are unsettling, void-born mimics of creation, not an organized race with cities or a language. They should look profoundly uncanny, not generic demons.
8. **Do not depict living dragons** or divulge the secret identity of the final Eclipse Avatar. Draven are draconic descendants, not surviving dragons.
9. Give each environment its Bible identity: **Gothic City** around a huge broken celestial fragment; **Ashen Forge** with Grimstarr workers and cooling molten rivers; **Verdant Deep** with immense living forest; **Sun-Sand Wastes** with buried sunlit ruins; **Frozen Crown** with silent icy peaks; **Floating Isles** with celestial debris and impossible waterfalls; **Eclipse Shadow** as a gradual reality wound, not cartoon black smoke.
10. When featuring a dungeon party or raid, respect the **latest user-authorized direction: five-player dungeon groups and ten-player raid teams**. This overrides older four-player and eight-player tables in parts of V6. Arena options include 1v1, 2v2, 3v3, 5v5 and 10v10 battlegrounds, but this is a cinematic reveal, not a UI-driven feature list.
11. Avoid any claim that trailer footage represents completed in-engine gameplay if it is pre-rendered or AI-generated. Use “Cinematic concept / Previsualization” labeling as appropriate; target platforms PC, Xbox Series X|S, PS5 are **planned**, not certified.

### Character and asset fidelity
- Every close-up face should have natural skin pores, melanin-correct subsurface scattering, micro-expression, eyebrow/eyelash detail, carefully shaped eyes, individualized facial proportions, and truthful light response without skin-lightening.
- Hairstyles need individually modeled locs/coils/braids with gravity, collision, motion, and readable silhouettes; no hair clipping through pauldrons or weapons.
- Armor needs practical layered construction: under-armor, straps, buckles, hinges, textile seams, metallic wear, dirt, forged engraving, realistic weight, and race-compatible fitting.
- Weapons must be distinct, believable ancient artifacts, **not oversized arcade icons**. Show edge geometry, grip wear, hammer dents, patina, forged steel, restrained internal magical seams. The iconic Vanguard Sunbreaker may appear as a recognizable relic greatblade.
- Spells must emanate from an actual cast source; illuminate skin, armor, dust, water, smoke, and architecture; cast shadows where physically appropriate; create contact effects and aftermath rather than floating decorative particles.
- Combat must communicate momentum, spacing, anticipation, dodge, parry, guard impact, foot placement, recoil, stagger, injury reactions, stamina, and deliberate attack timing. Every hit must connect spatially. No weightless spinning, clipping hands, or random explosions with no cause.

### Cinematography and look
- Filmic realism, fine atmospheric depth, rich blacks without losing detail, controlled highlights, volumetric clouds/fog, cinematic exposure, atmospheric particles, natural color contrast, believable scale references.
- Camera grammar: slow establishing dolly, aerial crane, intimate 50–85 mm portrait shots, low-angle relic close-up, grounded shoulder-level tracking during combat, carefully chosen slow motion only at emotional turning points. No constant frantic camera shake.
- Use restrained environmental audio during the opening; deepen into orchestral strings, low brass, real percussion, and an original wordless choir, then a powerful final cadence. Original music only: no copied WoW theme, melodies, or recorded sound effects.
- No floating gameplay HUD, damage numbers, menus, XP popups, loot labels, or fake developer logos in the cinematic. In-world armor and glyph text must be consistent with the art bible.

## Narrative concept: “THE LIGHT BROKE. WE DID NOT.”
A world once created by the Aethel begins to lose its light; viewers fall in love with the civilizations and the peoples before seeing terrifying proof that creation is unraveling. A single Aetherborn protagonist, himself a falling fragment of the divine, stands against the spreading dark. The sequence evolves from sacred beauty to grief, dread, defiance, and extraordinary combat. The closing beat is a choice, not a spoiler.

### Shot-by-shot director's storyboard, approximately 120 seconds

**SHOT 01 | 00:00–00:09 | Before the wound**  
Begin on black and distant celestial resonance. A microscopic shimmer becomes the vast Aethel filling an almost silent sky above the ancient mountains. Extremely slow cinematic push in. No title. Voice: “Before the first crown… there was the Aethel.”

**SHOT 02 | 00:09–00:20 | What the light gave**  
Sunlight crosses the Gothic City built around a colossal cracked celestial fragment. Show bustling streets: Black Solari merchants, grim dwarven forge workers, different races and skin tones, moving cloth, bells, smoke from kitchens, children, distant monumental bridges. Camera floats across richly textured architecture to one intimate human face. Voice: “Its light gave shape to stone… and breath to every living thing.”

**SHOT 03 | 00:20–00:30 | The crack**  
An enormous hairline fracture crawls across the Aethel's face. No attacker appears; its cause remains unknown. Sunlight turns strange and cold; birds change course; a temple bell rings without a hand. The Gothic City crowd looks skyward. The music stops on a single low note. Voice: “Then… the light began to break.”

**SHOT 04 | 00:30–00:40 | Fires beneath the world**  
In the Ashen Forge, a Grimstarr master hammers a half-finished blacksteel blade. Extreme close-ups of real forging sparks, graphite, molten channels, ash, furnace texture, perspiration and cracked leather gloves. A river of molten stone visibly dims. Eyes in a soot-streaked face register terror. Voice: “Our forges cooled.”

**SHOT 05 | 00:40–00:51 | Beauty at the edge**  
Move through the Verdant Deep in a serene long take: giant roots, bioluminescent moss, insect wings, suspended pollen, rain on leaves, drifting forest mist. A Kazari hunter with intelligent feline features and authentic lived-in gear senses something approaching. A quick cut reveals Frozen Crown glaciers and a sun-sand ruin under ominous clouds. Voice: “Our oldest lands began to forget us.”

**SHOT 06 | 00:51–01:04 | The fallen celestial**  
HERO REVEAL: silhouette of a muscular Black male Aetherborn on a suspended stone bridge in the Floating Isles, deep-brown skin, carefully modeled long locs moving in the high wind, intricate yet physically believable dark armor. Orbit from behind to a 70 mm close-up of his eyes and jaw. His **violet-indigo fracture markings** light up under the skin, and **matching geometric wings of celestial electricity** unfurl from his back, releasing physical sparks and lighting suspended stone fragments. Not feathers. Not static decorative wings. Voice: “And from the falling heavens… some of us awoke.”

**SHOT 07 | 01:04–01:14 | The other peoples**  
A brief lyrical montage of unique faces: a proud Black Solari woman with sculpted twists and scarred plate armor; a Vyrael seer with exhausted ancient eyes; a horned Hellsworn; a scaled Draven warrior; a spectral Necrevan keeping vigil; a towering Tharuk; a Nereyth near moonlit sea, and a Myceliar emerging through fungal roots. Do not turn this into a character-selection grid. Each look needs cultural authorship and individuality. Voice: “We are not one blood… but this world is ours.”

**SHOT 08 | 01:14–01:25 | Something wearing our shapes**  
At the boundary of the Eclipse Shadow, an ordinary-looking person turns and their face shifts into an impossible void-mimic contour; a Fractured emerges from a seam in space. A dread hush, cloth flutter, involuntary human reaction. No generic monsters doing generic lunges. Voice: “The darkness does not hate us. It simply takes what the light leaves behind.”

**SHOT 09 | 01:25–01:37 | The first strike**  
Grounded hero-on-monster combat: a Vanguard braces, catches a huge blow on shield, iron sparks flash, he staggers backward, adjusts his feet, and counters with Sunbreaker. An Umbral Assassin runs low along the creature's blind side; a Riftwalker creates one brief afterimage, not a constant portal storm. Show real contact, recovery frames, stone fragments, believable weight and excellent timing. Percussion enters.

**SHOT 10 | 01:37–01:47 | Magic changes reality**  
A caster freezes a collapsing arch; an allied fire spell hits it and shatters the frost into hot vapor and falling ice. A Stormcaller releases lightning into flooded stone causing branching current across water, reflected in armor and faces. Show physically coherent spell origins and environmental interactions. Music surges; no screen-covering particle fog.

**SHOT 11 | 01:47–01:55 | United**  
A **five-hero** dungeon group navigates the Weeping Foundry; cut to a **ten-hero** raid bracing beneath the impossible vaulted stone of the Cathedral of Unmade Hours. Two fast arena flashes show an intense duel and a 5v5 tactical confrontation with distinct team roles. These are cinematic representations, not proof of implemented content. Show discipline, class synergies, shielding, healing and coordinated attack arcs.

**SHOT 12 | 01:55–02:00 | What will remain**  
Our Aetherborn stands at a precipice before the wounded Aethel. His wings dim to thin strands of light as the sky's fracture spreads silently in the distance. Hard cut to black on a final heartbeat. One last close whisper: “What will you become?” Reveal original title **ECLIPSE: REALMS OF THE FRACTURED** and tagline **THE LIGHT BROKE. WE DID NOT.** End card: **MOMENTUM LABS | IN DEVELOPMENT | FOLLOW THE PROJECT**; if listing platforms, explicitly label **TARGET PLATFORMS: PC / XBOX SERIES X|S / PLAYSTATION 5**. No invented launch date or platform-partner insignia.
