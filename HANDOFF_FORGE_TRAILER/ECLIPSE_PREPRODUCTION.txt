## 4. ART DIRECTION LOCK

### Materials
- **Steel:** Aged, forged, dark alloys, patina, wear, battle scars, tool marks
- **Stone:** Carved, weathered, cracked, moss-covered, ancient
- **Cloth:** Woven, layered, straps, buckles, seams, wind physics
- **Skin:** Pores, melanin-correct SSS, micro-expressions, sweat, dirt
- **Hair:** Individual strands, gravity, collision, motion, no clipping through armor

### Color Palette
- **Base:** Deep shadow crush (RGB 12,8,18), rich blacks without losing detail
- **Midtones:** Amethyst/violet (hue 270, sat 40%), indigo, silver
- **Highlights:** Gold (hue 45, sat 70%, lum 65%), warm amber, dying corona light
- **Contrast:** Warm gold vs cold blue, warm forge vs cold frost, holy light vs void shadow

### Magic / VFX Rules
- ONE magical accent per idea — not everything glowing simultaneously
- Spells emanate from actual cast source
- Light illuminates skin, armor, dust, water, smoke, architecture
- Cast shadows where physically appropriate
- Contact effects and aftermath — not floating decorative particles
- No screen-covering particle fog

### Rejection Rules
REJECT shots with:
- Inconsistent Aetherborn faces
- Feathery wings
- Washed-out dark skin (no skin lightening)
- Cheap glowing plastic armor
- Oversized cartoon weapons
- Unconvincing feet-on-ground movement
- Broken fingers
- Duplicated NPCs
- Drifting symbols
- Glowing effects that cast no light
- Spells passing through objects without consequence
- Gameplay UI, damage numbers, menus
- Motion artifacts, temporal inconsistency
- Copycat imagery from reference trailers

## 9. TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

### Delivery
- **Master:** 4K UHD 3840x2160, 24fps, 16:9, H.264/H.265
- **Cutdowns:** 60s, 30s, 15s
- **Audio:** 5.1 surround + stereo mix
- **Captions:** SRT file
- **Stills:** 12 keyframe stills (one per shot)

### Generation Pipeline
1. Generate at 720p native using Wan 2.2 TI2V-5B (50 steps, bfloat16)
2. Upscale to 4K using ffmpeg (Lanczos + hqdn3d + curves color grade)
3. Assemble 12 shots in order
4. Add voiceover, music, sound design
5. Final color grade (deep shadow crush, amethyst/gold)
6. Export master + cutdowns

### Quality Gates (per shot, 0-5 scale, must average 4+)
1. Originality
2. Craftsmanship
3. Material realism
4. Animation feel
5. Spell spectacle
6. Readability
7. Representation (authentic Black characters, textured hair)
8. Performance
9. Integration
10. Canon compliance
