SECTION 1 — THREE-PART SONIC GRAMMAR (the project's musical identity)
================================================================================

The ECLIPSE soundtrack is built on a three-part sonic grammar. Every cue must
serve at least one part; the main theme weaves all three together.

----------------------------------------------------------------
PART A — THE AETHEL (the leitmotif)
----------------------------------------------------------------
- An ORIGINAL five-note motif that sounds luminous yet incomplete.
- Must be produced as a documented, rights-cleared SEED: a human-performed
  MIDI or recorded instrument reference — NOT left to text generation to
  reproduce a tune.
- Must survive different instrumentation and harmonies (solo cello, full
  orchestra, detuned Fracture texture, etc.).
- Design exercise: create 3 fully original five-note candidates from an
  approved human musician or MIDI sketch. Select by blind listening, mood
  match, and recognizability. Preserve motif ID, notated pitches, intervals,
  rhythmic cells, key-independent MIDI, BPM reference, stems, ownership chain,
  and transformation notes. Name it MOTIF_AETHEL_01 only when approved.
- Acceptance test: a person who has never heard the theme can hum the
  signature motif after two listens, and the melody still works on solo cello
  with no percussion or choir.
- DO NOT copy or prompt a melody from any existing soundtrack.

----------------------------------------------------------------
PART B — THE LIVING PEOPLES (acoustic specificity)
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Acoustic detail, physically performed rhythm, distinct regional ensembles.
- West African-inspired percussion is an explicit V6 canon direction — but it
  is NOT a substitute for cultural specificity.
- If commissioning performers: identify precise instruments, traditions,
  credits, and context.
- Avoid a single undifferentiated "tribal" sound applied to every race.
- The 13 proposed ancestries each deserve a distinct instrumental voice woven
  into one coherent harmonic language (see M11).

----------------------------------------------------------------
PART C — THE FRACTURE (a texture, not a melody)
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Partially erased overtones, detuned bells, sound arriving before its
  physical source, sub-bass drones, reversed reflections, breath-like distant
  voices, and selective silence.
- Dissonant and dimensional — NOT "evil creature" horror music.
- Do NOT bury gameplay cues beneath wall-to-wall dissonance.
- Must layer cleanly with gameplay threat pulses.

----------------------------------------------------------------
MIXING PHILOSOPHY
----------------------------------------------------------------
- A game-world soundtrack should often be QUIETER than the player expects.
- Environments need air for dialogue, footsteps, and combat telegraphs.
- Reserve maximum orchestral impact for irreversible narrative beats, major
  boss phases, and the cinematic crest.

SECTION 2 — MAIN TITLE THEME (paste-ready)
================================================================================

TITLE: The Last Light of the Aethel
SETTING: Suno Custom mode | INSTRUMENTAL ON | target 3-4 minutes (edit to fit)

--- BEGIN PASTE ---
Compose an entirely original, emotionally unforgettable dark-fantasy orchestral
game main theme for ECLIPSE: REALMS OF THE FRACTURED, a world shaped by a
celestial creative force whose light is slowly fracturing. Monumental symphonic
dark fantasy, sacred cosmic wonder, tragic grandeur, intricate acoustic
ensemble, distinct West African-inspired hand-drum interlocks performed with
human groove, layered djembe and dunun alongside deep cinematic bass drums,
passionate solo cello, intimate low strings, soaring but restrained violins,
warm French horns, dark brass, harp, spectral bowed glass, celestial bell
partials, distant wordless mixed choir, subtly detuned resonance, and very
restrained analog sub-bass. Start almost silent with a fragile, memorable
original five-note melody; introduce suspended harmonies and measured
percussion; build an emotionally devastating orchestral statement, then return
to solitary unresolved tones. Dynamics matter more than loudness. Original
melodic construction, varied arrangement, musical space for character dialogue
and foley. Wonder > reverence > dread > grief > defiance > fragile hope.
Realistic acoustic depth, controlled low end, organic ensemble detail,
beautiful quiet passages, no pop structure, no EDM/trap, no intelligible
lyrics, no endless repetitive ostinato, no generic trailer booms, no imitation
of recognizable films or games.
--- END PASTE ---

ACCEPTANCE TEST: Someone who has never heard the theme should be able to hum
its signature motif after two listens, and the melody must still work on solo
cello with no percussion or choir.
