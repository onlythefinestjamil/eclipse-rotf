R E A D T H I S F I R S T
How Hermes must use this handoff
This PDF starts with a new, authoritative art and implementation addendum and then includes the prior
61-page V3 production bible unchanged as an archival appendix. V4 resolves or flags earlier conflicts; for
topics not superseded here, V3 remains a design reference. Nothing in this document certifies that a playable
game, console access, 3D models, or finished visual effects already exist.
Governing priority: Deliver a cohesive original, visually astonishing yet playable dark-fantasy game.
The user wants an Elden Ring-caliber sense of beauty, mystery, physicality and detail, not copied
FromSoftware assets, locations, characters, signature weapons, maps or animations.
Direction locked: cinematic realistic dark fantasy, aged natural materials, rare supernatural color,
extraordinary silhouette design, lived-in ruins and visually legible combat.
Direction rejected: recent weapon catalog images looked too much like WoW, with excessive gold trim,
symmetry, inflated weapons and always-on neon glows; do not reproduce that look.
Character creation locked: a complete Black avatar experience with deep skin-tone variation, locs,
freeform locs, braids, twists, cornrows, Afros, fades, waves and high-quality hair physics.
Production requirement: every concept must be expressed as an engine-ready asset spec with sources,
scale, geometry, materials, UVs, rig, FX, collision, LODs and tests.
Combat priority: fluid action chains, intentional timing, cast bars, hybrid short GCD / individual
cooldowns, manual critical windows and defensive execution, CC diminishing-return UI built in.
Community/PvP/PvE: 1v1 / 2v2 / 3v3 / 5v5 arenas, battlegrounds, 5-player dungeons, 10-player
dungeons and raids, separate PvE / PvP ranking systems, loot rarity, talent trees and set bonuses.

W O R L D B E A U T Y S Y S T E M
Environment art: macro, meso, micro
Three scales of world composition
Macro (30-300 seconds of travel): silhouette the skyline; one strong landmark per vista; terrain layers,
waterways, elevation, framing, color temperature, weather fronts, route anticipation, hidden verticality.
Create compositions players recognize from afar.
Meso (3-30 seconds of exploration): architectural language, traversable gates, broken bridges,
shrines, inhabited market textures, roads, foliage masses, navigation cues, ambient creatures, sound
zones and encounter geometry.
Micro (0-3 seconds of close inspection): chisel marks on stone, copper corrosion, sculpted reliefs,
textile weave, boot wear, moss rooting in mortar, jewelry patina, hand-written shrine script and regional
craft signatures.
Six region-specific visual bibles
SOLARA / THE CROWNLANDS
Black limestone, warm tarnished brass, ochre street dust, white-gold light on old metal; layered citadels,
crowded trade avenues and reservoirs; architecture bears scars from eclipsed solar rites.
Hero shot: a loc-haired Black knight stands at a rain-glazed gate; the black-iron cathedral and distant
farmland frame a silent eclipse.
VAELWOOD / LIVING WILDS
Enormous trees with believable growth plates, translucent resin, fungi, leaf scatter, ancestral stone
observatories, rope-and-root engineering, low mist and living biome sound.
Hero shot: a root bridge spirals around a tree wider than a city plaza, with sunshafts and slow-moving pollen.
MORVATH / ASH DOMINION
Basalt citadels, volcanic glass, sooty bronze, orange furnace pockets in charcoal landscapes, ash eddies,
mining scars, chain elevators, heat distortion.
Hero shot: a dwarfed traveler passes an abandoned colossal foundry whose chimneys exhale memory-
charged vapor.
AETHERIS / SHATTERED SKY
Levitation structures must have visible logic: tension rings, broken aqueducts, unstable anchor pylons, cloud
shadows and gravity fractures; only select fracture seams glow.
Hero shot: a muscular dark-skinned Aetherborn with controlled energy wings stands on a broken floating
viaduct.
NHALOR / FORGOTTEN NORTH
Blue-gray glacial rock, sculpted ice stratification, matte fur and metal, frost on seams, frozen observatories,
light scattering through ice and strong silhouettes in whiteouts.

Environment dressing rules
Every region needs a reference board of real-world geology, historic construction techniques, biome
ecology, cultural craft and original supernatural motifs.
Make traversal readable via silhouette, lighting, material contrast, landmark placement and sound, without
painting arrows on every surface.
Use handmade hero locations and modular kits for repeated buildings; ensure every modular seam is art-
directed in prominent frames.
Author progression variants: peaceful / damaged / occupied / fractured; use level/data layers only with
tested quest-state persistence.
Vegetation is not wallpaper: foliage bending, debris lift, leaf motion and shadow responses react to wind,
weather and select attacks.

Aetherborn / Lunarian hero production brief
Create a muscular Black male Aetherborn with deep, warm-brown skin and meticulously modeled
shoulder-length locs, a calm but formidable face, dense wing-muscle/upper-back anatomy, and handcrafted
segmented dark-iron armor. His fracture markings are luminous veins embedded in the skin, not flat painted
tattoos; during flight, each line extends into skeletal light-ribs that unfold as translucent energy wings,
refracting light and shedding subtle fragments. The wings retract fully when grounded and their glow respects
dark-skin shading, nearby armor and weather. They must not obscure gameplay camera, hit silhouettes, PvP
telegraphs or allied spell effects.

22 | Risk register and unsolved decisions
The following must remain visible rather than silently buried by ambitious feature generation.
• MMO scope risk: enormous authored content and backend operations; default to shared hubs and instanced
co-op until funding and retention justify a seamless persistent world.
• Ten-client performance: hair physics, VFX and replicated combat traffic compete for frame budget; establish
concurrency/performance tests early.
• Class balance: 24 specializations multiplied by 1v1,2v2,3v3,5v5 and PvE is a large tuning surface; simulate
matchup telemetry and phase content rollout.
• Art throughput: original realistic playable characters require sculpting, rigging, animation, LOD, clothing
adaptation and human QA; AI pictures are concepts only.

12 | Crowd control, PvP balance and competitive
integrity
Eclipse proposed crowd control: Stun, Disorient, Fear, Root, Silence, Knockback, Displacement, Slow and Blind.
Define shared diminishing-return category taxonomy; hard CC must be expensive enough that a team cannot
permanently prevent control of one player.
• Prototype DR option A: first full category CC, second 50% duration, next immune until a 16-second category reset
(inspired by 2026 WoW changes). Test against alternative three-step DR; do not claim balance until empirical data.
• Hard CC duration caps and standardized break-free resources; unbreakable chains prohibited. Give primary healer
meaningful interrupt and escape counterplay.
• Arena healing dampening begins after a defined elapsed time and increments visibly; threshold depends on
bracket. Favor predictable rules over secret anti-stall effects.
• Burst window design: powerful group sequences should be readable through sound, VFX, HUD and telegraphs,
with counters such as dispel, line-of-sight and proactive defense.
• Stacking restrictions: unique haste aura, damage resistance cap, speed cap, vulnerability cap, damage-to-heal
cap, summon cap and reflect chain prevention.
• PvP-only modifiers stored by ability ID/spec and patch version. Gear item-level normalization, banned PvE proc list,
competitive trinkets and stats budgets.
• Ranked abuse prevention: server validated damage/movement/inventory, bot detection, network anomaly flags,
match-fixing review, reports, bans and appeals.
• Ratings: hidden skill MMR and visible rank/rating, separate queues and ladders by bracket, team size matched,
seasonal rollover and transparent rewards.
• Accessibility and fairness: color-blind readable effects, captioned sound signals, high-contrast team outlines and
no purchasable competitive advantage.
